## Research intake

For each important question, record the exact source locator, sample/venue, data availability, evidence family, applicability, independent counterevidence and unresolved limits. Separate market rules, published observations and proposed effects. Preserve conflicting versions in reference order.

Begin with the master plan’s primary-source references and the repository source register. Prioritize research that can change the baseline, data eligibility, costs or choice of falsifier. No market-specific claim has been accepted in this scaffold.
